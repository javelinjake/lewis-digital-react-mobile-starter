# Family Moves: connected school-to-home designs

Three static concept boards, 2 October 2026. Use the warm orange identity: burnt orange #B94716, apricot #F3B58A, warm white #FFF9F3, charcoal #342B27 and sand #EADDD0. Family Moves remains a working name.

## Family app

The primary loop is choose → try together → return when useful. Today offers one suitable activity and two alternatives. Activity instructions work without watching a video. Seated options appear with the activity. Saved supports quick repeat use. Our moments is a private optional memory, with no scores or streaks. An adult-only collection area explains one-off purchases and confirms everyday activities stay free. The Rainy-Day Moves price is a hypothesis.

## School platform

Teachers choose year, unit and lesson. The workspace brings together the objective, setup, equipment, timings, teaching cues, adaptations and observation prompts. Short video is a reference; it does not substitute for the lesson. Essential lesson PDFs and matching home cards remain accessible. Paid staff support is optional and separate. No pupil leaderboard or household monitoring dashboard is proposed.

## PE resource kit

A4 delivery plan: compact usable structure, setup diagram and learning cues. Station card: a large movement illustration and three short prompts. A5 home card: familiar activity, household equipment, clear instructions, seated choices and optional digital continuation. The generated QR is explicitly a placeholder and must be replaced with a tested activity URL before production. Create editable print masters and verify grayscale, type size, margins and print legibility before publishing.

## Connected example

Teacher opens Passing & catching → delivers a reviewed lesson → prints Sock pass home invitation → household tries it from the card or direct browser link → adult optionally saves an activity or explores more ideas. No account or paid purchase should be required to use the invitation activity.

## Motion direction for implementation

Use a short 150–200 ms fade when changing activity options; keep layout stable. A gentle illustration highlight can demonstrate the pass on request, with a static equivalent. Confirm saving with a brief icon change and text. Avoid automatically looping motion, countdown pressure, confetti and animated progress targets. Honour reduced-motion preferences. Teacher setup diagrams can reveal stations step by step using explicit controls and printable static diagrams.

## Next design iteration

Turn the family home and activity detail, school lesson workspace and home card into editable designs. Test five parents and five teachers on finding an activity, understanding adaptations, opening a lesson and printing the home version. Prioritise comprehension and time to usefulness. Review lesson content with a qualified PE educator; fictional illustrations and example timings are not validated pedagogy.
