# Family Moves: orange direction

Initial brand and app concept · 2 October 2026

[Full-size board](family-moves-orange.png) · [Brand comparison gallery](index.html#orange)

**Brand line:** Small moves. Healthier days.

A clear, energetic family fitness and wellbeing identity. Burnt orange is the primary brand/action colour, balanced by apricot, warm white, sand and charcoal. This direction uses a new wordmark and abstract moving-figures logo concept, with no electric-blue UI or earlier logo.

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Burnt orange | `#B94716` | Logo and primary buttons, with white button text |
| Apricot | `#F3B58A` | Supporting accents, with charcoal text |
| Warm white | `#FFF9F3` | Page background |
| Charcoal | `#342B27` | Body text and headings |
| Sand | `#EADDD0` | Supporting surfaces |

The deeper orange is intended to support readable white action labels; bright apricot is for accents rather than white-text buttons. Final UI contrast and raster colour reproduction require implementation checks.

## Screen ideas

1. **Start:** a five-minute family movement suggestion, with Move / Eat well / Feel good visible and one clear Let's move action.
2. **Participate:** standing/seated choices, one short instruction and an easy early finish. Different family members can lead.
3. **Finish:** acknowledge the shared moment, optional honest feedback, Save this idea and Done for today. No score or catch-up obligation.

Typography is friendly, bold and readable. Illustrations show activity in familiar family rooms rather than gym performance. Motion could use a short card transition and small one-shot orange accent; reduced motion would retain the same content/actions. This board is static and does not implement animations.
