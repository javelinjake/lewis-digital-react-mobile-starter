# Games

A game is an app with a `core` package and a server that owns the clock.

## `core`: the rules

Pure functions of `(state, config, now, seed)`. No React, Capacitor, Payload, `Math.random` or `Date.now` (the architecture check enforces it). Randomness comes from `@ld/utils/random/rng` (`createRng(seed)`, `fork`, `poisson`): the same seed gives the same result on every device and on the server.

The app imports `core` to **preview** (a lineup score, a win chance, an "upgrade" badge). The api imports it to **decide**. Because it is the same code, the numbers always agree.

Every number lives in a config object validated by a schema, edited in the admin as a global, and stamped on results. Tuning needs no app release.

## Time

The server owns every clock. Store timestamps (`lastCollectedAt`), not counters. On every open and resume the client calls `/game/sync`, and the server computes what happened while away from its own clock. Changing the phone's clock does nothing.

The key property to test: `advance(advance(s, t1), t2)` equals `advance(s, t2)`. The game pays the same whether the player checks in every five minutes or once a day.

Tests and playtesters can skip time with the `X-Debug-Now` header, honoured only when `ALLOW_TIME_TRAVEL=1` (refused in production). In mock data mode the app runs the rules in the browser on a clock Playwright moves with `window.__pixelPitchClock.advance(ms)`.

## Rewards and cheating

A reward endpoint is a `defineAction`. The client sends what it did; the server recomputes the outcome from the seed and clamps the reward to what was possible. A mini-game gets a single-use ticket with a seed and a maximum payout; `finish` checks elapsed time and score against it.

## Pixel Pitch's systems

Everything below is a pure function in `apps/pixel-pitch/core`, run by the api (the real outcome) and by the app's mock mode (development, Playwright, Lighthouse), so the two cannot disagree.

| System | Where | How it is kept honest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Footballers | `footballers.ts`, `catalogue.ts` | A fixed set of invented players with stable ids (`FOOTBALLERS`, the same as the api's catalogue migration), never generated again: merging needs copies of one player and the album needs gaps to fill. `cards.retired` takes a player out of packs and the album (`packPool`) without touching cards anyone owns; the launch set is 200, 180 in packs (60 Grassroots, 50 Semi-Pro, 40 Pro, 22 Star, 8 Legend). A season's new set of about 30 goes on the end with new ids, in the admin and in `footballers.ts`; older sets stay in packs. The album deals each nation's players, best first, into sets of six, and the few left over from every nation into the Internationals: 30 sets with every pack player in one. |
| Packs | `flow-packs.ts` | A roll is seeded by the club and how many packs it has opened, never by the client. A Star or better is guaranteed within 40 players. The shelf makes one pack every 4 hours and holds 3 (5 with the Pass); with the daily free pack, one coin pack a day and an Arcade pack (450 tokens, about a day's arcade) an engaged player gets about 20 players a day. The bench holds 20. A pack is held as `pendingPack` until each player is signed or sold. Open all shows each pack's three players face up and opens the next on Done. |
| Stadium, tray | `flow-stadium.ts`, `flow-arcade.ts` | A pile is derived from a timestamp (`stadiumCollectedAt`, `trayCollectedAt`), never stored as a counter; collecting moves the timestamp. The arcade has one tray, not one per game. |
| League | `league.ts` | Matches play themselves every three hours, each from its own seed, so a match played late gives the same result as on time. A win is 3 points, a draw 1 and a loss 0, and every result pays coins (600, 400, 250), so a losing club still grows through training and packs. Each league asks more points to go up than the one below (`league.promotionPoints`: 30, 36, 42, 50, 60, then +10 a Prestige tier; `promotionGate`), and rivals are 7 rating points stronger each league. No relegation. Past League 1 come the Prestige tiers: each is another league with a gate 20 higher and rivals 2 rating points stronger (never past `league.rivalCap`, 96, so a top squad can still win), and each one reached is for good: a star on the crest, a line in the trophy cabinet (`trophies`, every promotion with its date) and +2% coins from matches and the stadium (`prestige.coinBonusPerTier`, `withPrestige`). Nothing the player has collected is ever reset. `pnpm --filter @ld/pixel-pitch-core pacing` plays clubs through the real rules for weeks of game time and prints how many days each kind of player takes to reach each league: run it after changing any league, pack or economy number. |
| Match moments | `moments.ts`, `play-config.ts` | A Big Chance is banked (`takeMoments`, in every catch-up) from a league match the player missed that ended level or a goal down: a penalty (Penalty Shootout), a free kick (Kick Timer) or a save (Keeper Reflex, only in a one-goal loss), its kind and minute drawn from the club and the match. The bank holds 5; the newest replaces the oldest. A match that kicked off at most `moments.liveWindowMinutes` (5) before the player looked in was watched: it offers one live moment instead, whatever the score, never both. A moment is one round of its game (`playConfig`), started with a ticket (`/game/chance/start`, which takes it out of the bank: one try) and finished with the taps (`/game/chance/finish`), replayed by the server. Scored, the result moves up a step (a loss to a draw, a draw to a win) and the difference in points and coins is paid; the stored match is rewritten (`changeFixture`: a goal for us at the moment's minute, or their goal turned into a save) so its report tells it. Points only while the club is still in that league, coins either way; reaching the gate promotes there and then. Missed or let go (`/game/chance/skip`: Let it play, or no tap within `moments.decideSeconds`) costs nothing. In the app the match report stops at a live moment's minute, and Skip to full time stops there too. |
| Difficulty | `config-minigames.ts` | Every game is `minigames.difficulty.perLeague` (8%) quicker in each league above League 5, up to `max` (60%): speeds multiplied, times divided (`minigamesAt`). A ticket records the club's league when the play started, and the app draws the game and the server replays it at that league's pace (`playConfig`, the app's `PlayTuning`). Rounds and targets do not change, so a top score is the same in every league. |
| Mastery | `mastery.ts` | Every arcade game has bronze, silver and gold targets in every league, as shares of its top score (`mastery.shares`; the games are quicker each league, so the same share is harder higher up). A finished play records the best in that game and league (`arcade.bests`, keyed `cabinet:tier`) and any medal reached (`arcade.medals`); the first time each medal is reached it pays once: bronze 500 coins, silver 80 tokens, gold 15 gems. A play bought with gems keeps its best but earns no medal, because silver pays tokens and gems must never turn into the tokens that buy random packs. Tokens are still paid in proportion to the score. |
| Daily challenge | `challenge.ts`, `play-config.ts`, api `actions/challenge.ts`, `collections/challenge-entries.ts` | Every day every player gets the day's featured game in endless mode (`endlessMinigames`: Kick Timer, the penalties and Keeper Reflex end at the first fail; Keepy-Uppy and Dribble Dash just lose their limits; every game speeds up past anyone's reactions, so there is no perfect score to tie on), from the same seed (`challenge:<day>`), at League 5's pace for all. One attempt a day, free: it counts as played from the start. The day's pattern is only playable in that attempt. Taking part pays 25 tokens, and each of the day's bronze, silver and gold targets beaten pays too. The api stores one entry per club per day with the club's look, and answers the boards (`/game/challenge/board`) with only public parts: name, crest, kit and score. One board for everyone at launch. A day's result is 0 to 100 by position; a week's score is the best five days (`weeklyTable`). Last week's band is paid when the player asks (`/game/challenge/weekly`, from the week's final table): top 3 a Star pack and the podium badge, top 10% a Star pack and gems, top half gems and a pack, three days played a pack; bands do not stack. Server replay proves a score happened, not that a person made it: a score above `challenge.ceiling` or taps more even than a person's (`judgeEntry`) is stored with `flagged` and kept off everyone else's board until an admin ticks `reviewed`. Attempts are never sold. |
| Arcade energy | `energy.ts` | One bar for every game: 5 (6 with the Pass), one point back every 90 minutes. Only the time the bar is full again is stored (`energy.fullAt`), never a counter, so it refills on the server's clock whether or not anyone is looking and the Pass raises the ceiling at once. A play costs 1. With none left, 20 gems buy a play that pays coins and Pass XP, never tokens. |
| Arcade games, warm-up | `minigames.ts`, `arcade-games.ts`, `arcade-taps.ts`, `flow-arcade.ts` | Five games, each about 15 to 30 seconds and each a different skill: Kick Timer, Keepy-Uppy, Penalty Shootout, Keeper Reflex (reactions) and Dribble Dash (dodging). The server issues a ticket with a seed and the time. The phone sends only what the player did, in each cabinet's own shape (`CabinetTaps`: tap times for Kick Timer and the penalties, a step and a place for each Keepy-Uppy tap, a time and a place in the goal for each Keeper Reflex tap on the ball, the lane changes); the server replays it with that seed (the game is a pure function of both) and checks its own clock ran at least as long as the play says. `perfectTaps` plays any cabinet perfectly, for the tests. Tickets are single use. Taps closer than 120 ms are refused, and Keepy-Uppy locks the player out for half a second after a mistimed tap, so mashing does not work. A best score is kept for each game, and one game a day (taking turns) pays +50% tokens. The warm-up before a league match is a free play of one of the five, drawn from the club and the match (`warmupCabinet`, so it is the same game however often the player looks): `finishWarmup` takes that game's taps, scores them with `scorePlay`, and turns the share of the top score into the next match's attack bonus (80% or more +10%, 45% or more +5%, anything else +2%; `minigames.warmup` and `match.warmup*`). It costs no energy and pays no tokens, coins, best score, goal progress or Pass XP; one try a match. (Pass Master, Target Practice and Cone Shuffle were taken out: `upgradeState` drops a saved club's best scores for them and an unfinished play of one, and an old config's sections for them are ignored.) |
| Claim all | `flow-claim-all.ts` | `waitingToClaim` says what is waiting (stadium, tray, calendar day, free pack, goals, Pass tiers, album sets). `claimAll` takes the idle rewards (stadium, tray, free pack, album sets) by running the individual claims one after another in one transaction, so the Ready now bar and the claim cannot disagree. Finished goals, the login streak's day and League Pass tiers are left for the player to claim on the Goals and League Pass screens. `/game/claim-all`. |
| Goals, calendar | `flow-rewards.ts`, `progress.ts` | The day's goals are drawn from the club id and the date, from a pool that includes taking a Big Chance, playing the daily challenge and earning a medal. Progress is counted as things happen (a win only counts on the day it was played). A claim is checked against the state, never against what the phone says. The login streak pauses over a missed day rather than starting again, and day 7 pays three Star packs, more than a normal day's packs. |
| League Pass | `flow-rewards.ts` | 28-day seasons from a fixed date (so every player is in the same season). XP from matches, packs, arcade plays, goals, the daily challenge (10) and each new medal (5). A late Pass unlocks every tier already reached. |
| Album | `album.ts` | Sets are dealt from the catalogue (each nation's players of each position, best first, dealt like cards into sets of six, so every set mixes top and bottom). Each finished set is +1% team power, applied in `scoreLineup`, so it is in the preview and the match alike. |
| Match report | `commentary.ts`, `commentary-lines.ts` | A few big moments, not every event: the kick-off, every goal, up to six chances in all (a quiet match is topped up to four with saves and misses that change nothing on the scoreboard), a little colour, half-time and full-time. A chance has two beats, what is happening and then what came of it. Fourteen kinds of goal, five of save, seven of miss and ten colour incidents, each with its own lead-ins and finishes, plus a rare own goal. The goals and the score after each are the match's own, so the report always agrees with the result. |

A club's state (`ClubState`) is JSON in the `clubs` collection. A saved club from an earlier version gains any new field through `upgradeState`, so adding to the state never needs a data migration. (The one that changed shape: the two arcade token trays became one, emptied at the earlier of the two times, and the daily plays became energy, starting full.)

### Accounts and devices

There is no sign-in screen. A first launch makes this device its own account (a Better Auth guest, kept in the Keychain or Keystore on native, a cookie on the web) and goes straight to founding a club. The club lives on the server like any other. Settings offers **Save to the cloud** (create an email account; the club moves to it) and **Sign in to the cloud** (play an existing account's club here; the device's club is deleted, because an account has one club). A device-only player cannot sign out, which would lose the club. Signing out of a cloud account starts a fresh device club, with a sign-in link on the New club screen.

### Where things are

Five tabs: Club, Packs, Squad, Arcade and League, each with a count of what is waiting. Settings is a button in the header. The top of the Club screen is **Ready now**, when there is something to claim: one chip for each reward (a chip goes to the screen that has it) and one button, **Claim all**, that takes them and shows what it paid. Goals, the login streak and League Pass rewards are not in it: their tiles and the header's Goals button carry a count, and the player claims them on those screens. The end of a match, an arcade play and a claim each offer the next step, the pack opening has **Reveal all** and **open the next pack**, and Welcome back has Claim all. Merging three copies of a player shows what changed: stars, rating, training kept, sell value and squad places freed.

### Screen sizes

Phones are portrait. On tablets and desktop browsers the whole screen (header, content, tab bar) is a centred column (`MobileShell column`, `max-w-xl`), and on iPad the app rotates, since iPadOS no longer supports portrait-only apps. Android tablets keep the portrait lock below Android 16, which ignores it on large screens; the column handles either.

### Numbers that are placeholders

Every number is in the game config (an admin-editable global), so these can be tuned after a playtest without an app release. The ones that are guesses and not in the MVP spec: stadium curve and upgrade costs; the arcade tray rate (24 an hour, 8 hours) and tokens a play (up to 60); arcade energy (5, 6 with the Pass, one back every 90 minutes) and the +50% for the game of the day; gems for an extra play (20) and what it pays (300 coins); the tuning of all five arcade games (speeds, zone widths, twists, gravity, ramp, reach, Keepy-Uppy's drift and the size of the ball a tap must touch, the penalty ball's wander and the keeper's reach, Keeper Reflex's shot waits, flight times and the size of the ball a tap must touch, defender pace); the match report (six chances at most, four at least, two colour moments, and the reading time for each beat); the warm-up's thresholds (80% and 45% of a game's top score); the goal pool and its rewards; the calendar and Pass rewards; XP for each source (match 2, win +1, pack 2, arcade play 3, goal 15); the tactical-edge table; rival strength; training caps and costs; win and draw coins; the middle rows of the pack odds table.

Three to look at first in a playtest: the arcade energy (is five plays and 90 minutes a point back too tight or too loose?), Keepy-Uppy's difficulty (a play lasts 30 seconds at most; a player who reacts to the zone opening rather than anticipating the ball gets about 12 kicks, a quick one about 30), and the Pass pacing. XP is set so a player who does everything every day (8 matches, 9 packs, 6 arcade plays, 3 goals: about 100 XP a day) just fills the 3,000-XP track in a 28-day season, and a casual player reaches about tier 16. Change `pass.xp` or `pass.xpPerTier` if playtesters finish early or never get past the first week.

## Game kit (`@ld/game`)

- Atlas loader for TexturePacker "hash" JSON, `Sprite` and `AnimatedSprite` (whole-number scaling, `image-rendering: pixelated`).
- Key-colour recolouring: `#ff0000` becomes the club's primary and `#00ff00` the secondary (`swapColours`). Cache the result.
- `createFixedStep` and `useCanvasLoop`: fixed-step logic, optional fps cap, stops while the page is hidden.
- `createAudioManager`: separate music and effects switches, mutes music in the background, unlocks on the first tap.
- `useReducedEffects` (`@ld/react-utils`): low-effects mode for weak devices and reduced-motion users. One class on the root; every feature still works.

## Formations and the match (Pixel Pitch)

The spec's `xG = 1.35 × (ATT ÷ their DEF)²`, with ATT divided by 2 and DEF by 3, could not produce counter-picks. A ratio squared makes a side's strength one number, ATT × DEF. Every role's weights add up to 1, so that product is highest for the most balanced formation: Blitz was the best reply to everything and Wall was never worth picking. The rule now is:

- `ATT = Σ(slot × att weight) ÷ 2.5` and `DEF = Σ(slot × def weight) ÷ 2.5`, both × (1 + chemistry). ATT + DEF is always twice the lineup score, so a formation moves strength between attack and defence and never adds any.
- `xG = baseXg × 2^((ATT − their DEF) ÷ doublingGap)`, capped at `maxXg` (defaults 2.4, 20, 8). The gap counts, not the ratio. Two Boxes of equal players expect the spec's 1.35 goals each.
- A tactical-edge table (`formations.edges` in the config) gives one formation a few rating points over another: added to its ATT and DEF and taken off the other side's. The defaults are placeholders: two cycles (Wall > Blitz > Diamond > Wall by 5; Diamond > Box > Pyramid > Blitz by 2) and 1-point edges for the other pairs.

Five 70-rated players a side (win % for the row against the column):

| | Box | Diamond | Pyramid | Wall | Blitz | Average points | Goals each, mirror | Best reply to it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Box (2-2) | 37 | 29 | 41 | 33 | 34 | 1.99 | 1.34 | Diamond |
| Diamond (1-2-1) | 45 | 35 | 30 | 44 | 18 | 1.98 | 1.10 | Blitz |
| Pyramid (2-1-1) | 27 | 36 | 29 | 24 | 45 | 2.01 | 0.62 | Box |
| Wall (3-1) | 28 | 18 | 28 | 22 | 52 | 2.01 | 0.34 | Diamond |
| Blitz (1-1-2) | 44 | 63 | 29 | 19 | 41 | 2.01 | 2.40 | Wall |

Scored 3 for a win, 2 for a draw and 1 for a loss (so 2 is an even record), every formation averages 2 points against the field, and the best reply changes with the rival's formation. Without any edge, defending pays against a stronger side (against a +7 rival: Wall 1.84 points, Blitz 1.71) and attacking pays against a weaker one (against −7: Blitz 2.31, Wall 2.19). `bestFormation(squad, rival)` ranks the five for the squad screen's hint. `tests/match.spec.ts` checks the balance, the counter-picks and the stronger/weaker rule, so a config change that breaks them fails CI.

### Sound

Sound effects from the asset pack (`art/source/audio/sfx`), converted to mono AAC `.m4a` by `art:build` and precached by the service worker with the app shell. `src/lib/sound/sound.ts` plays them with Web Audio, each fetched and decoded the first time it is wanted: the pack tear, card flips and a sting by rarity, the kick-off and full-time whistles, our goals and the result, every arcade hit and miss, medals and new bests, claims, promotions, match moments, and a quiet tap on every button. Settings has a switch (kept on the device). The music loop is not used yet.
