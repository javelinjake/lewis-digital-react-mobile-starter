# Competitor landscape: casual mobile football card-collection / idle club-management games (as of 25 Nov 2026)

Reading notes for the report writer:
- "Company-reported" = stated by the developer/publisher (press release, earnings, interview). "Third-party estimate" = modelled by AppMagic, Sensor Tower, AppGoblin, MWM etc. and can be wrong by a large factor, especially for small apps.
- AppGoblin figures are free, modelled estimates given in coarse bands ("$10K+", "<$10K", "$100K+" per month); treat them as order-of-magnitude only. Two reads of the same AppGoblin Retro Bowl page in this session returned different numbers (see below), which is itself a signal of how noisy they are.
- Sensor Tower "top 5 soccer games" country/quarter blog posts are auto-generated single-country weekly snapshots; they cannot be summed into global totals.
- Paywalled dashboards (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, data.ai, Similarweb) were not accessible; Companies House filings were not opened. See Gaps in each section.

## Direct comparables (pack-opening / card-collection / football manager games): models, scale, performance

### Takeaway
The category is barbell-shaped: three or four licensed giants (FC Mobile ~$15m/month, eFootball at 1bn cumulative downloads, Top Eleven, DLS) take almost all the money, while the pack-opener apps closest to Pixel Pitch's core loop (MADFUT and similar) get large audiences but monetise almost entirely through ads rather than IAP, and depend on mimicking EA's Ultimate Team card data. Blockchain/NFT football collecting (Sorare, Matchday, FIFA Rivals) has shrunk or stayed small.

### Inferences
- Pack-opening apps prove demand for the pure "open packs, build a squad" loop with zero match gameplay, but the evidence found shows them monetising via ads, not IAP (MADFUT 26 lists no IAP at all). A no-ads, no-paid-packs game like Pixel Pitch has no direct revenue precedent in this sub-niche; its season pass + gems model is closer to the manager games (Top Eleven, OSM) than to the pack openers.
- MADFUT-style apps are parasitic on EA's Ultimate Team: annual re-release, real player cards. Their audience is FUT fans. A fictional-player game cannot rely on the same "pull Mbappé" motivation and should not assume MADFUT-scale installs.
- Even the manager incumbents appear to lean on ads for a large share of revenue (AppGoblin's modelled splits: Top Eleven 61% ads, OSM 77% ads). If directionally right, "no ads yet" leaves a meaningful share of typical genre revenue on the table.
- In July 2026 the two most-downloaded football games were unlicensed (Soccer Superstar, Football League 2026), ahead of FC Mobile and eFootball. Licences are not required to win downloads; they matter more for revenue.
- Sorare's four-year revenue slide and the small download counts of FIFA Rivals suggest "ownership"/NFT framing adds little; collecting as a free-to-play loop is the durable part.

### Gaps
- PacyBits and DEVCRO (FUT draft/pack-opener apps): no download, revenue or status data was found in this session. Not verified whether they are still published in 2026.
- Soccer Stars and Football Strike (Miniclip), Score! Match: no figures found.
- No global monthly revenue estimates for Top Eleven, OSM, DLS, Score! Hero, eFootball or MADFUT from AppMagic/Sensor Tower dashboards (paywalled). Only single-country snapshots and AppGoblin bands.
- MADFUT 26 install count: Similarweb data is paywalled; Google Play listing was not read.
- Matchday's actual current activity (DAU, revenue) is unknown; "live" status rests on a third-party listing and a Play Store event snippet.
- IAP price lists were only read for Retro Bowl and MADFUT 26 (UK). Season pass prices for Top Eleven, FC Mobile, OSM, DLS were not collected.
- First Touch Games, Nordeus and Gamebasics headcount and company accounts were not retrieved (other than Gamebasics' 60 staff in 2021).

## Indie / retro / pixel sports comparables: earnings, team size, monetisation

### Takeaway
Retro Bowl is the standout indie benchmark: 7m+ downloads with zero UA spend by Oct 2021 and ~$3m estimated revenue by Nov 2021, monetised by a £0.99 one-off unlock plus small credit packs and no ads. But its football sibling Retro Goal did roughly 2% of that revenue in its first five months and still earns under ~$10K/month by third-party estimate, so the same studio, style and model applied to soccer produced a far smaller result.

### Cited Findings

**Retro Bowl (New Star Games, UK; founded by Simon Read in 2003)**
- Company-reported (29 Oct 2021): over 7 million downloads, more than a third of them in the preceding month; nothing spent on user acquisition; 4.8 score from nearly a million reviews; #1 free app on iOS in the US — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/news/77425/uk-based-new-star-games-takes-us-by-storm-with-retro-bowl/)

Note the conflict: AppMagic's 3.4M downloads vs the company's 7M+ three weeks earlier; the company figure also counts web play via Poki or AppMagic under-counted.
- Monetisation (UK App Store, read 25 Nov 2026): free; "Unlimited Version" £0.99; Coaching Credits packs from £0.99 (x25) to £29.99 (x2000); no ads — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/retro-bowl/id1478902583)
- AppGoblin, 2026 (third-party estimates, inconsistent between reads): one read showed ~9.7M installs, ~147.7K MAU and "$50K+" monthly revenue; another read on 25 Nov 2026 showed 10.3M installs, ~106K MAU, "$10K+" monthly revenue, 100% IAP / 0% ads, 4.62 stars from 516K ratings — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.newstargames.retrobowl)

### Inferences
- Retro Bowl's success was distribution-driven (App Store feature + TikTok virality in Sept 2021, US market, NFL has few mobile competitors), not model-driven. The same studio's football game in the same style landed two orders of magnitude lower early on. Football on mobile is far more crowded than American football, so "be the Retro Bowl of soccer" is not a safe base case; Retro Goal is the realistic reference.
- Retro Bowl's revenue per download was low: ~$3M over 3.4M–7M downloads implies roughly $0.40–$0.90 per install lifetime-to-date in 2021 (calculated from the two sources above). A £0.99 unlock caps ARPU; Pixel Pitch's recurring 28-day pass has a higher ceiling per payer but needs retention that Retro Bowl (by AppMagic's account) did not have.
- Indie pixel sports games in this set monetise without ads and without loot boxes (Retro Bowl, Retro Goal, Kairosoft premium, FM Mobile via Netflix). "No ads, no paid random packs" is consistent with what this audience already accepts; the differentiator for Pixel Pitch is adding a live-service pass on top.
- Idle Eleven suggests an idle football tycoon can reach ~10M Android installs yet still sit in the roughly $10K–$50K+/month band on third-party estimates: large install base, modest revenue.
- FM Mobile moving behind Netflix removes the premium-priced serious manager from the open store, leaving the "pay once" slot for football management less contested on mobile.

### Gaps
- New Star Games Ltd Companies House accounts (turnover, profit, headcount) were not retrieved; no team-size figure for New Star Games was found. Co-development of Retro Goal with Five Aces Publishing implies a small core team but this is not quantified.
- No current authoritative Retro Bowl lifetime downloads or revenue (post-2021). The 2021 figures are five years old.
- No data found for: New Star Soccer / New Star Manager, Pixel Cup Soccer (Batovi), Football Chairman (Underground Creative), Club Boss, Soccer Tycoon, "Tiny Football", Pocket League Story specifically (price, downloads).
- FM Mobile on Netflix: no download or player numbers published that were found.
- Retro Bowl College figures not collected.

## Revenue distribution in the niche: top-grossing vs mid-tier vs long tail

### Takeaway
Mobile game revenue is extremely skewed: in an April 2026 catalogue of ~44,000 monetising mobile games, the median earned about $462/month and roughly 59% earned under $1K/month, while the top 1% earned ~$1.2M/month. In football specifically, the observed tiers run from ~$15m/month (FC Mobile) through ~$100K–$500K+/month (established managers) down to under ~$10K/month for most indie and niche collecting apps.

### Cited Findings
- MWM catalogue, state of April 2026, mobile games with at least 100 downloads in the last 30 days that monetise: median $462/month; top 1% ~$1.2M per 30 days. Bucket counts: under $1K: 26,186 games; $1K–10K: 9,792; $10K–100K: 5,434; $100K–1M: 2,301; $1M–10M: 457; $10M+: 57 (third-party estimate) — [MWM](https://mwm.ai/glossary/mobile-game-monetization)
- The same source gives an ARPDAU range of $1–5 for "Sports / racing" (third-party; this looks high for casual sports and is unexplained on the page) — [MWM](https://mwm.ai/glossary/mobile-game-monetization)
- AppMagic "The First $1M" study: only 158 mobile games released between 1 Jan and 13 Oct 2026 passed $1M lifetime IAP revenue by 17 Oct 2026 (excluding 36 China-led titles). 48% of IP-based games in the sample reached $1M within 30 days vs 6% of originals; median time to $1M was 35.5 days for IP-based and 83.5 days for originals. Sports was not broken out — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/mobile-games-first-million-2026)
- Older survey evidence: half of 252 iOS developers had made less than $3,000 lifetime revenue per app; a 2016 analysis put 57% of mobile developers under $500/month per game — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/data-and-research/33895/survey-of-252-developers-finds-half-have-made-less-than-3000-lifetime-revenue-per-app-on-ios/); [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/app-stores-steam-and-indie-poverty-the-web-as-an-alternative-)
- Football tier datapoints (all third-party estimates, cited in the sections above): FC Mobile $15m in Aug 2025 ([PocketGamer.biz](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/the-state-of-eas-mobile-portfolio-following-its-55bn-acquisition/)); Top Eleven "$500K+"/month on Android ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/eu.nordeus.topeleven.android)); OSM "$100K+"/month on iOS ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/400201466)); Retro Bowl "$10K+" to "$50K+"/month ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.newstargames.retrobowl)); Retro Goal "<$10K"/month ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.newstargames.retrogoal)); Topps Total Football "<$10K"/month ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1565131075/trends))

### Inferences
- From the MWM bucket counts (total 44,227 games): ~59.2% earn under $1K/month, ~22.1% earn $1K–10K, ~12.3% earn $10K–100K, ~5.2% earn $100K–1M and ~1.2% earn $1M+ (calculated). Only about 1 in 5 monetising games clears $10K/month. The sample already excludes games with fewer than 100 monthly downloads and games that do not monetise, so the true distribution for all releases is worse.
- A realistic prior for a solo-developed, unlicensed, non-viral football game with no UA budget is the under-$1K/month bucket; reaching the $1K–10K bucket (where Retro Goal and Topps Total Football appear to sit) would already be an above-median outcome. $10K+/month puts a game in roughly the top fifth.
- Original (non-IP) games take longer and are much less likely to break out quickly (6% vs 48% within 30 days among those that reached $1M at all), which fits the licensed-vs-unlicensed split seen in football.
- A rough tiering for football on mobile: top (FC Mobile, eFootball: $10m+/month), upper-mid (Top Eleven, DLS, OSM, Football Rivals: roughly $100K to low $ millions/month), lower-mid (Retro Bowl, Idle Eleven, MADFUT via ads: roughly $10K–$100K+/month), long tail (Retro Goal, Topps, most indies: under $10K/month). The band boundaries are inferred from the coarse estimates above, not from a single dataset.

### Gaps
- No sports- or football-specific revenue distribution dataset was found; the percentile data is for all mobile games.
- No figure for what share of all newly released games ever reach $1M (AppMagic explicitly does not report this).
- No source found giving typical day-1/day-7/day-30 retention or payer conversion for casual football games specifically.

## Gaps and positioning opportunities; what users complain about

### Takeaway
The open space is a non-FUT, fictional-player, ad-free collection game with real (if light) gameplay around the pack loop: the pack openers have collection but no IAP model and depend on EA card data, the managers have monetisation but a serious tone and ad load, and the pixel indies have charm but thin long-term retention. The complaint evidence gathered here is thin and mostly about pack-opener friction (duplicates, trading, scams) rather than systematic review mining.

### Cited Findings
- MADFUT 26 UK App Store critical reviews centre on quality-of-life friction: duplicates must be quick-sold one card at a time for ~40 coins, store packs cannot be opened in bulk, trades fail because the other player leaves, and users ask for coin trading to return "with safeguards against scams" — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/madfut-26/id6752884808)
- MADFUT has an off-platform real-money grey market (code/pack shops) with scam complaints, despite the app itself listing no IAP — [Trustpilot madfutkofut](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/madfutkofut.com); [Trustpilot madfutshop](https://nl.trustpilot.com/review/madfutshop.com)
- Retro Bowl reviews: main asks are more depth and difficulty; a minority complaint about feeling pushed to buy credits, disputed by other reviewers — [App Store UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/retro-bowl/id1478902583)
- Retro Goal's original hard paywall on career mode was dropped in favour of free career + optional paid unlimited mode (Feb 2023), and its rating remains the weakest of the New Star titles at 3.94 — [PocketGamer.biz](http://www.pocketgamer.biz/f2p-scores-a-win-with-retro-goals-monetisation-strategy-changes/); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.newstargames.retrogoal)
- Pokémon TCG Pocket drew player backlash over its trading system and pull rates (headline of Kotaku coverage) — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-pocket-dena-players-trading-hourglass-pull-rates-2000722438)
- FM Mobile on Netflix markets "no ads or in-app purchases" as a feature — [Netflix Tudum](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/football-manager-26-mobile-game-news)
- FIFA Heroes positions as 5-v-5, 2-minute matches for "a new generation" — the same short-session 5-a-side framing as Pixel Pitch, but with the FIFA licence and action gameplay — [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/world-cup-2026-games-trends/); [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260624471996/en/FIFA-Heroes-launches-globally-the-cross-platform-mobile-first-football-game-built-for-a-new-generation-of-players)

### Inferences
- Positioning gaps that the evidence supports: (1) no found competitor combines pack collection + idle + auto-sim + pixel art in portrait; (2) the pack-opener audience demonstrably wants bulk actions, a fair way to dispose of duplicates, and safe trading — cheap features for a newcomer to get right; (3) "no paid random packs" is a credible trust and regulatory-safety message in the UK/EU, where the pack openers and FUT are the usual loot-box reference points, and it avoids the grey-market/scam dynamic MADFUT has; (4) fictional players remove licence cost and takedown risk (MADFUT 25 was removed from the US App Store, reason not stated).
- Risks the evidence supports: (1) fictional players lack the aspirational pull that drives FUT-style pack openers, so collection desire must come from art, rarity and sticker-album completion; (2) trading is both the most-requested and the most-complained-about feature in card collectors (MADFUT, TCG Pocket), so launching without it avoids pain but also removes a retention driver; (3) a hard paywall mid-progression was reversed by Retro Goal, suggesting generous free play with optional pass is the safer shape.
- Without ads or paid packs, revenue depends on pass conversion from a small install base; given the distribution above, the business case should be tested against the under-$1K/month scenario.

### Gaps
- No systematic review mining was done. Complaints about pay-to-win in Top Eleven, OSM, FC Mobile and DLS, and about ad load in pack openers and OSM, are widely asserted but were not sourced in this session and should be treated as unverified.
- PacyBits and DEVCRO review themes not collected.
- No evidence gathered on UK/EU loot-box regulation status in 2026 or on store policy for odds disclosure; another researcher may cover this.
- Reason for MADFUT 25's removal from the US App Store is unknown (could simply be the annual app being retired).

## 2025–2026 trends: market size, World Cup 2026 effect, FIFA/EA licence, card-collection genre

### Takeaway
The overall mobile games market is flat (+1.3% IAP revenue in 2025), World Cup 2026 delivered a real but short download spike that favoured free, lightweight and often unlicensed football games, FIFA's post-EA multi-studio licensing has produced several small arcade titles rather than a new giant, and Pokémon TCG Pocket showed a free daily-pack collection loop can be enormous but decays quickly (roughly $40–45m/month in 2025 to ~$25m/month in H1 2026).

### Cited Findings

**Market size and growth**
- Sensor Tower: mobile game IAP revenue was $81.75bn in 2025, +1.3% YoY (third-party estimate) — [GameDev Reports summary of Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026?open=false); [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/01/21/in-2025-the-revenue-of-the-mobile-gaming-market-grew-but-only-slightly-analysis)
- Male players dominate sports games, with significant gender gaps in sports, strategy and shooters (Sensor Tower, via summary) — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026?open=false)
- EA's mobile segment is shrinking year on year (Q3 FY26 $254m, -8% YoY) even as FC Mobile is cited as its growth driver — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/ea-mobile-revenue-falls-to-254-million-in-q3-fy26)

**World Cup 2026 effect**
- AppMagic benchmark from the 2022 World Cup (US, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain): sports game downloads +54% across the tournament, peaking at +71% during the finals, while net revenue rose only +24% during the finals; FC Mobile was the biggest beneficiary in 2022 — [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/world-cup-2026-games-trends/); [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-world-cup-numbers-dtc-hits-17bn-uefn-payouts-mays-top-ads-more/); original analysis at [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/fifa-effect-on-mobile-apps)
- July 2026 actuals (AppMagic estimates): Soccer Superstar 14.2m downloads (+45% MoM), Football League 2026 12.4m (+32%), FC Mobile 11.4m (best since 2022), eFootball 11.2m (+27%), Dream League Soccer 7.7m (best since Feb 2020) — [PocketGamer.biz July 2026 charts](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/july-2026-mobile-game-charts/)
- The football titles gathered momentum up to World Cup final day (19 July 2026) and all dropped off afterwards — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/julys-top-mobile-game-downloads-roblox-free-fire-arrows-block-blast-soccer-superstar-more/)
- Non-football hits (Gossip Harbor, Royal Match, Coin Master) ran World Cup live ops; Moon Active ran three World Cup-adjacent live ops at once — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-world-cup-numbers-dtc-hits-17bn-uefn-payouts-mays-top-ads-more/)
- Sports apps (not games): downloads +297% in the opening stage of major tournaments on AppMagic's historical data; revenue +18% at opening — [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/world-cup-2026-games-trends/)

**FIFA / EA licence changes**
- FIFA now licenses multiple third-party studios rather than one exclusive publisher; FIFA Heroes (Enver/Solace, June 2026) is described as the first title under the new model and FIFA's first mobile game since ending EA exclusivity; FIFA Rivals (Mythical, June 2025) is FIFA's official arcade title; Netflix released "FIFA World Cup: Launch Edition" (48 teams, 16 stadiums) — [Game Developer press release](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/fifa-heroes-launches-globally-the-mobile-first-take-for-the-new-generation-of-football-fans); [FIFA](https://inside.fifa.com/organisation/news/fifa-rivals-launches-worldwide-non-simulation-arcade-football-mobile-devices); [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/world-cup-2026-games-trends/)

**Card-collection genre: Pokémon TCG Pocket**
- AppMagic: $235.3m in its first full month (Nov 2024), still its best month; $1.3bn in its first year, more than Pokémon Go's first year by ~$245m — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/pokemon/pokemon-tcg-pocket-reportedly-made-usd245-million-more-than-pokemon-go-managed-in-its-first-year); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pokemon-tcg-pocket-makes-16bn-in-15-years/)

- DeNA data cited as showing the game losing players; player backlash over trading restrictions and pull rates — [Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/pokemon-trading-card-game/pocket-losing-players); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-pocket-dena-players-trading-hourglass-pull-rates-2000722438)
- Physical football collecting is having a cultural moment around the World Cup ("The return of the football collector", June 2026; Front Office Sports on card collecting going digital, Oct 2026) — [SoccerBible](https://www.soccerbible.com/features/2026/06/the-return-of-the-football-collector/); [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/sports-card-collecting-sets-its-sights-on-the-digital-age/)

### Inferences
- Pixel Pitch is pre-launch in November 2026 and has missed the World Cup 2026 window. The next comparable tailwinds are season starts each August and Euro 2028 (hosted in the UK and Ireland); the 2022/2026 pattern says tournaments lift downloads far more than revenue (+71% vs +24%), and the lift disappears within weeks.
- The download winners in July 2026 were lightweight free titles, two of them unlicensed. A tournament is an acquisition event for casual football games regardless of licence, but it rewards games already ranking in store search.
- TCG Pocket validates the core Pixel Pitch loop (free timed packs, collection album, light play, paid pass/premium) at mass scale, but its success rests on the strongest collecting IP in the world, and even it lost roughly 40% of monthly revenue within about a year. A fictional-roster game should expect weaker initial pull and plan content cadence (new sets) as the main retention lever.
- FIFA's multi-licensee strategy increases the number of branded casual football games competing for store featuring and search, including a direct 5-a-side arcade title (FIFA Heroes), though none has yet shown large scale.
- With overall mobile IAP flat, growth for a newcomer comes from share-taking, not market expansion.

### Gaps
- No sports- or football-genre market size (revenue or downloads, 2025 or 2026) was found in open sources; Sensor Tower's "Beyond the Game: The Sports Report 2026" is gated and its game figures were not retrieved.
- No measured 2026 World Cup revenue uplift for football games (only the 2022 benchmark and 2026 download counts). June 2026 chart figures were not extracted; one search summary flagged that the PocketGamer.biz June page may contain inconsistently labelled data.
- UK-specific download/revenue figures for football games during the World Cup were not found.
- Whether the EA–FIFA situation changed further in 2026 (e.g. any new simulation licence deal) was not established beyond the FIFA Heroes / Rivals / Netflix titles.
- Download and revenue figures for Netflix's FIFA World Cup: Launch Edition were not found.
